# The problem in numbers

Spanish copy: [es/impacto.md](es/impacto.md).

Every figure that the submission form, the README and the docs use to explain why the tool matters, with its year, its scope and its source. A research agent with web search found them, and every URL was consulted on 14 November 2026; the team left out the ones the agent could not confirm (see “Not used” below). A person has not yet re-opened every page behind these figures ([ai-use.md](ai-use.md)).

None of these figures enters the engine or the traffic light: they describe the problem, not the plots. The figures of our own validation (2018 test, POWER against CHIRPS, 2026 observation) are in [validation.md](validation.md).

## Not used

The research turned up other figures that could not be confirmed, so they appear nowhere in the project:

- A percentage and a number of people in IPC Phase 3 or above for Chiquimula alone.
- A count of households for the Dry Corridor.
- A percentage of WhatsApp use among farmers.
- An “official threshold” for the length of the canícula: we use none, and we call our cut-off a team decision.
