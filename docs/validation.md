Limits:

- It is an observation of the past, not a forecast. The 2026 rain and soil-moisture figures are an observation only: the climate record the engine scores with is 2001–2025. The one place 2026 does enter is the fire record, where detections from January to May 2026 count.
- Preliminary: the Climate Hazards Center recomputes CHIRPS every month, and SMAP can be reprocessed. The 2026 months come from the final product folder (not from prelim/); the date of each file is stored.
- SMAP: 3 cells of 9 km with data (4 plots; Jocotán and San Juan Ermita share a cell). Camotán has no data of recommended quality. With 12 years, "the lowest" is all that can be said; no percentile is given.
- The plots are example points chosen from satellite imagery, not verified in the field.

## Notes and limits

- CHIRPS is not a ground measurement: it blends satellite and stations. According to its README, CHIRPS v3 already blends INSIVUMEH stations (https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS/v3.0/README-CHIRPSv3.0.txt), so an independent comparison against stations is still missing.
- POWER: 0.5° × 0.625° cell (~50 km). CHIRPS: 0.05° (~5 km).
- The engine's rain is the CHIRPS monthly total split across days with POWER.
- High risk = the year's deficit is above the 75th percentile of the previous years (from 2001).
- NDVI signal = July-September anomaly of -1 standard deviation or less.
- SMAP: SPL3SMP_E.006 through AppEEARS, recommended-quality data only. Camotán has none; Jocotán and San Juan Ermita share the 9 km cell. SMAP measures the top 0-5 cm of soil and describes about 9 km of landscape, not the plot.
- The 5 plots are example points chosen with NDVI and Sentinel-2 imagery, not verified in the field; see [es/memoria/](es/memoria/Inicio.md).
